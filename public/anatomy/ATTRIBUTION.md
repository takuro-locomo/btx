The anterior JPEG is unmodified. The Sobotta PNG was converted to JPEG at the original dimensions for efficient delivery; the anatomical content was not altered. The application may crop the viewport or overlay educational region selectors. The anterior surface plate does not expose the corrugator separately; the lateral dissection plate has muscles cut away. These plates are reference anatomy, not patient-specific models or injection maps.
